Pocket Football Tactics

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Football Tactics handles information when you use the mobile game.

Effective date: September 27, 2026

1. Overview

Pocket Football Tactics ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a mobile football puzzle game focused on short tactical challenges, progression, stars, club customization, and related gameplay features.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information necessary to operate and improve the App.

2. Information We Collect

The current version of Pocket Football Tactics does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address in order to play.

Game-related information may be stored locally on your device, including:

  • completed levels and chapters;
  • stars and gameplay progress;
  • coins or other in-game progression values;
  • club name and customization preferences;
  • settings and other gameplay preferences.

This locally stored information is used to preserve your progress and improve your gameplay experience.

3. Device and Technical Information

Depending on your device, operating system, app store, or services integrated into the App, limited technical information may be processed automatically. This may include device type, operating system version, app version, crash information, language, and general diagnostic information.

Where such information is processed by Apple, Google, or another platform provider, it is also subject to that provider's own privacy terms and settings.

4. How We Use Information

Information handled by the App may be used to:

  • save and restore your gameplay progress;
  • manage levels, stars, rewards, and club customization;
  • maintain App functionality and performance;
  • identify and fix technical problems;
  • protect the App against abuse or misuse;
  • improve gameplay, usability, and future features.

5. Local Storage

The App may use local device storage to save game progress, settings, preferences, and other gameplay information.

Removing the App, clearing application data, or resetting your device may permanently delete locally stored progress unless a future version introduces an optional cloud backup or account system.

6. Third-Party Services

Pocket Football Tactics may be distributed through third-party platforms such as Google Play and the Apple App Store. These platforms may collect or process certain information independently in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Future versions of the App may also use services for areas such as analytics, crash reporting, cloud saving, advertising, or in-app purchases. If these services are added and materially change how personal data is processed, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Advertising and In-App Purchases

If advertising or in-app purchase functionality is introduced, the relevant advertising network, payment platform, Google Play, or Apple may process information necessary to provide those services.

Payment information is normally processed directly by the applicable app store or payment provider and is not provided to us in full.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or improve the App, or when required by applicable law.

9. Data Retention

Locally stored gameplay data remains on your device until it is deleted, overwritten, cleared by the operating system, or removed when the App is uninstalled.

If future online services are introduced, associated retention periods will be described in an updated version of this Privacy Policy where required.

10. Children's Privacy

Pocket Football Tactics is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children. If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected in a manner that is not permitted by applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information may contact us using the contact information below.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide rights relating to personal information, such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to certain processing.

Because the current version primarily stores gameplay information locally on your device, you can generally remove that information by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

12. Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect information handled through the App. However, no software, device, storage system, or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. International Users

The App may be available in multiple countries. Privacy and data protection rights can differ depending on your location. We aim to handle information in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes, when new services are introduced, or when legal requirements change.

The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
